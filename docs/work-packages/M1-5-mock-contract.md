## Stage 3: contract suites
- **Files:** `tests/contract/suites/{m1-browse,m1-library,m1-queue}.mjs`. Call every M1 method, validate against `protocol/schema` with ajv, and assert one semantic each (test-guide §2 style).
  - `library.window offset=4900 limit=200` → 100 items, `total` 5000.
  - `userAlbum.move` with a stale revision → `conflict`.
  - `history.window` groups are present.
  - `search.suggest` cancellation: the first call is `cancelled`.
  - The `queue` snapshot is first; `seq` increases.
- **`live` tests** (real target only, network): `home.get` without fixture, `search.run "sơn tùng"`, `album.get`/`artist.get`/`playlist.get` on the stable ids in `tests/contract/fixtures/live-ids.json` (choose long-lived official YouTube Music ids for a popular VN artist; record each with its URL), `charts.get zing`. Plus the **auto-advance test**:
  1. `player.play tracks [yt1, yt2]`
  2. seek to `dur − 3 s`
  3. track becomes `yt2` within 10 s
  4. then `[yt2]` alone with autoplay on: `queue.changed` total > 1 within 20 s of the end
- **Real-target tolerance:** the real core answers `track.setSource` and `import.files` with `unsupported`. The suite accepts that for exactly those methods.
